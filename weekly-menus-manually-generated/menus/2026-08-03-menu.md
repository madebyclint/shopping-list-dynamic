## Deeper View

**Spaghetti with Jarred Sauce** *(Monday, August 3)*
Total time: ~25 min · Approx calories: ~600 per adult

> Full meal day — ground beef browned and simmered into jarred marinara, tossed with spaghetti, side salad for the veg, parmesan on top.

**Ingredients Used**

- Ground beef (1 lb)
- Jarred spaghetti sauce
- Spaghetti noodles
- Bagged salad kit/greens
- Parmesan cheese, grated

**Cooking Overview**

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil; cook spaghetti noodles per package directions.
2. While noodles cook, brown ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks; drain excess fat.
3. Stir jarred sauce into the browned beef; simmer 5–10 min to let flavors meld.
4. Drain noodles, toss with sauce, and top with grated parmesan.
5. Serve with a side salad from the bagged kit.

**Air Fryer Garlic-Butter Wings & Steamed Artichokes** *(Tuesday, August 4)*
Total time: ~30–35 min · Approx calories: ~650 per adult

> Runs longer than the usual Tuesday fast-night target, but it's mostly unattended — wings crisp in the air fryer while the artichokes steam. Garlic butter ties both together. Cantaloupe on the side keeps it light.

**Ingredients Used**

- Chicken wings (2 lb)
- Whole artichokes, large (2)
- Butter
- Fresh garlic
- Cantaloupe (from fresh fruit)

**Cooking Overview**

1. Trim artichoke stems and snip sharp leaf tips; steam whole in a covered pot with a couple inches of water, 30–40 min until a leaf pulls away easily.
2. Pat wings dry, season with salt and pepper, and air fry at 380–400°F, 20–25 min, shaking the basket halfway, until crispy.
3. Melt butter with minced garlic; toss wings in the garlic butter right out of the fryer.
4. Serve artichokes with extra melted garlic butter for dipping.
5. Slice cantaloupe on the side.

**Maggi Braised Beef Ribs** *(Wednesday, August 5)*
Total time: 8 min cook (pressure cooker) · Approx calories: ~700 per adult

> House favorite — always all-beef short ribs, never substituted (Julian's preference). Braised with Maggi seasoning until fall-off-the-bone tender. Kimchi and orange slices on the side.

**Ingredients Used**

- Beef short ribs (2 lb)
- Maggi seasoning sauce
- Kimchi, store-bought
- Oranges (2)

**Cooking Overview**

1. Season short ribs generously with Maggi seasoning sauce, working it into the meat.
2. Pressure-cook (Instant Pot or similar) on high pressure ~35–40 min with a splash of water/broth, then natural release; the "8 min cook" reflects hands-on active time, not total pressure-cook duration.
3. Reduce the braising liquid on sauté mode if a thicker sauce is wanted.
4. Serve ribs with kimchi and sliced oranges on the side.

**Beef Bacon & Peas** *(Thursday, August 6 — TEEN PREP)*
Total time: ~20 min · Approx calories: ~550 per adult

> Teen-cooked night — simple and hard to mess up. Beef bacon pan-fried, peas heated through, boxed mac and cheese on the side, apple slices to round it out.

> 💡 **Teen note:** Start the mac and cheese water first — it takes the longest. Fry the bacon while the peas heat in a separate small pot. Slice apples last.

**Ingredients Used**

- Beef bacon (8 oz)
- Frozen peas
- Boxed mac and cheese
- Apples (from fresh fruit)

**Cooking Overview**

1. Boil water for the boxed mac and cheese; cook per box directions.
2. Pan-fry beef bacon over medium heat until crisp, 6–8 min; drain on paper towels.
3. Heat frozen peas in a small pot with a splash of water, 3–4 min; drain.
4. Plate bacon and peas alongside the mac and cheese, with sliced apples on the side.

**Seattle Dogs with Grilled Onions & Kimchi** *(Friday, August 7)*
Total time: ~20 min · Approx calories: ~600 per adult

> Beef uncured hot dogs (pork-free per house rule), Seattle-style with grilled onions, kimchi, and cream cheese on a toasted bun. Watermelon on the side.

**Ingredients Used**

- Beef uncured hot dogs (4)
- Hot dog buns
- Yellow onion (for grilling)
- Kimchi, store-bought
- Cream cheese
- Watermelon

**Cooking Overview**

1. Slice yellow onion and grill or pan-fry in a little butter/oil over medium heat until softened and browned, 10–12 min.
2. Grill or pan-sear hot dogs until heated through and lightly charred, ~6–8 min.
3. Toast buns; spread a thin layer of cream cheese inside each.
4. Build dogs with grilled onions and kimchi on top.
5. Serve with sliced watermelon on the side.
